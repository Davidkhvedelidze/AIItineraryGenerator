# Prometheus Cave: Tickets, Boat Ride & Visit Planning

Prometheus Cave makes an easy half-day addition to a Kutaisi stay, but two details can change the experience: the underground boat ride costs extra, and it may not operate on your visit. This guide covers tickets, transport, the walking route and a sensible November or winter plan, so you can decide whether the cave deserves a place in your Georgia itinerary.

## Is Prometheus Cave worth visiting?

Choose it if you enjoy natural formations and want a guided attraction without organising a mountain hike. The appeal is the succession of limestone chambers: hanging stalactites, formations rising from the floor and broad folds of rock picked out by artificial lighting. You are visiting a managed show cave, so expect a set route and other visitors rather than a private exploration.

Our recommendation is to make the decision around the walk. If an hour underground sounds interesting, the cave can justify the trip even without the boat. If the boat is your only reason for going, confirm its operation before leaving Kutaisi and keep another option available.

It also helps to compare the outing with your limited holiday time. For someone already staying in Kutaisi or Tskaltubo, the cave fits naturally. For someone spending only a few nights in Tbilisi, adding western Georgia solely for this visit creates a much larger transport commitment. Start with the regional route in our [Kutaisi travel guide](/blog/kutaisi-travel-guide-canyons-caves-and-georgias-second-city) if you are still deciding where to stay.

## Prometheus Cave tickets and the boat supplement

The [Agency of Protected Areas tariff page](https://www.apa.ge/en/eco-tourism/servisebi-da-tarifebi/Prometheuscave), checked on 4 November 2026, lists:

- Foreign adults: 40 GEL admission; 30 GEL extra for boating.
- Georgian citizens and residents: 16 GEL admission; 14 GEL extra for boating.
- Ages 6–18: 5.50 GEL admission; under six: free admission.

A foreign adult choosing both pays 70 GEL before transport. Guided entry is included. Confirm children's boat eligibility and charges separately; the child admission rate does not establish the boat price. Bring identification for any reduced-rate claim.

For two foreign adults, allow 80 GEL for the walking visit or 140 GEL with both boat supplements. Treat those as attraction costs, then add your actual transport quote and lunch. Comparing only an advertised transfer price with the admission ticket can make a supposedly cheap outing look better than it is.

Before paying a tour operator, ask for the inclusions in writing. Does the price cover admission, or just transport? Is the boat an optional payment at the cave? How long will the driver wait? These questions matter more than whether the product is labelled a “cave tour” or a “private excursion.”

For families, work out the total for each traveller rather than multiplying an adult package price by the number of seats. Keep a small budget margin for changed plans, but do not buy additional activities simply because you have already travelled there.

## Opening hours, Mondays and when to arrive

APA currently publishes daily opening from 10:00 to 17:00. The older [National Parks visitor guide](https://nationalparks.ge/en/site/prometheuscave/guide) still excludes Mondays. Because these official pages disagree, reconfirm Monday access and the final guided entry before travelling. The visitor centre's published number is +995 577 10 14 17.

Do not read a closing time as a promise that a full tour starts at that hour. We suggest arriving in the morning or early afternoon, with enough flexibility to wait for the next available group. That is a planning recommendation, not a confirmed departure timetable.

A useful message or phone enquiry contains your date, approximate arrival time, party size and whether you want the boat. If you need an English-language group, ask about that too. Checking four practical details together is more useful than a general “Are you open?” enquiry.

Avoid making the cave your final stop before a flight. You would be combining uncertain group timing, a possible boat decision and a road transfer with a fixed deadline. On departure day, choose a plan that leaves the airport buffer intact even if you skip the attraction.

## What the walking visit actually involves

The cave is near Tskaltubo in western Georgia. The [Kutaisi tourism office's overview](https://kutaisi.travel/en/5905/prometheus-cave-en/) describes a roughly kilometre-long visitor route and an interior temperature around 14°C. Think of it as a cool indoor walk with changes in level, not a warm-weather sightseeing stroll.

A light layer and shoes with dependable grip are sensible even when Kutaisi feels warm. Keep your hands free where possible, especially if you will also be helping a child. A small bag is more convenient than carrying loose jackets, shopping bags and a camera separately.

Allow about two hours at the site in your own schedule, rather than allocating only the guided walking time. This is a suggested buffer for tickets, gathering with a group and deciding on the optional boat; it is not a promise that every visit takes two hours.

If anyone in your party struggles with stairs, uneven surfaces or enclosed spaces, contact the visitor centre about their specific needs. Do not assume that a developed tourist attraction guarantees a step-free route. Similarly, ask about a pushchair before bringing one, rather than discovering its limitations after arrival.

For photography, stay with the group and stop where you are not blocking the path. The visitor guide permits photos and requires visitors to remain on marked routes. Use the experience to look at the formations as well as your screen; rushing between pictures can make the chambers feel surprisingly similar afterwards.

## Is the Prometheus Cave boat ride worth the extra cost?

The boat is an optional ending to the cave visit, not a substitute for admission. It offers a different perspective from the walking route, but it is best treated as a bonus.

The park's visitor guidance says boating stops when water levels rise. A rainy forecast therefore does not automatically make this a dependable all-weather boat excursion. Ask about the walking route and the boat separately: knowing that one is operating does not establish the status of the other.

For a couple weighing the extra cost, decide what you value. If you are excited by underground scenery from the water and the service is operating, the supplement may feel worthwhile. If you are keeping the day inexpensive, the main walking visit can be the whole activity without turning the outing into a compromise.

With children, confirm the operator's current participation rules and available equipment before buying the supplement. Free or reduced admission to the cave should never be interpreted as automatic boat access.

If your wider route also includes Martvili, avoid assuming the two boat experiences are interchangeable. One is connected with an underground cave visit; the other is a canyon outing. ## How to get from Kutaisi to Prometheus Cave

Set your destination to the Prometheus Cave visitor centre in Kumistavi, rather than simply to Tskaltubo. Use a current map estimate from your actual accommodation and add waiting time to the day's plan. A hotel in central Kutaisi and an airport pickup are different journeys.

### Taxi or a prearranged driver

For a straightforward half-day visit, a return arrangement is often the simplest choice. Request a quote that explicitly includes the cave, the waiting period and the return destination. If you want lunch or a Tskaltubo stop afterwards, include it in the original request.

Do not assume that a one-way ride comes with a driver waiting outside. Agree where you will meet after the visit and how to contact each other. Families should request suitable child seating before confirming the vehicle, especially if a transfer service offers several vehicle sizes.

### Rental car

Driving gives you control over the afternoon, but it is worth checking parking and the return route before setting off. Save the visitor-centre location while you have a reliable connection. Leave luggage out of sight and avoid planning a long onward drive simply because the cave visit itself is relatively short.

A rental car makes more sense when it supports several days of your itinerary. Hiring one only for a single nearby attraction may be less convenient once collection, parking and return arrangements are included.

### Public transport via Tskaltubo

Treat this as a journey that needs local confirmation, not as a guaranteed direct cave service. Ask about transport to Tskaltubo, the onward connection to the visitor centre and, crucially, your return options.

We are not publishing an unverified bus number or timetable here. If nobody can confirm the last leg and the return, compare the full combination with a prearranged ride before committing. Lower initial fares are less helpful if the final connection requires an unexpected taxi anyway.

## A relaxed half-day itinerary

For a first visit, we recommend pairing the cave with Tskaltubo rather than filling the day with several distant attractions.

- Morning: leave Kutaisi after confirming opening and boat status; arrive with time to join a guided visit.
- Late morning: take the walking route, adding the boat only if available and wanted.
- Lunch: leave a proper break instead of eating around a tight transfer schedule.
- Afternoon: choose a short Tskaltubo stop or return to Kutaisi for city sightseeing.

These are suggested parts of the day, not reserved slots. The benefit is flexibility: a delayed departure changes lunch timing instead of forcing you to abandon a second prepaid attraction.

If Tskaltubo interests you, read our [Tskaltubo visitor guide](/blog/tskaltubo-abandoned-sanatoriums-complete-guide) before choosing stops. Historic resort buildings are not all public attractions; stick to permitted access and avoid treating an open doorway as an invitation.

For travellers who prefer a separate canyon-focused day, TripMate's [Canyons Tour in Imereti](/tours/canyons-tour-in-imereti-region) is a bookable alternative departing from Tbilisi. Its published itinerary covers Martvili, Okatse and Kinchkha; it does not include Prometheus Cave. Keep that distinction in mind when comparing it with a Kutaisi-based cave outing.

## Visiting in November or winter

Prometheus Cave can suit a cooler-season western Georgia itinerary, especially when you want variety alongside city walks. Its cool interior means you should still dress for the underground visit independently of whatever the outdoor forecast says.

However, indoor scenery does not remove weather from the decision. Check the operating status after heavy rain, and avoid organising the whole day around an assumed boat departure. A city afternoon or a shorter local outing is an easier backup than racing to a distant attraction.

With shorter daylight, make the cave your main scheduled stop and keep optional outdoor sightseeing afterwards modest. Families travelling at a slower pace will usually get more from one complete visit and an unhurried lunch than from collecting several entrances in a single afternoon.

Before leaving your accommodation, confirm opening, boat operation, return transport and the needs of every traveller. Once those are settled, you can spend the visit enjoying the chambers instead of solving logistics at the ticket desk.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Prometheus Cave cost?

The checked tariff is 40 GEL for foreign adults, plus 30 GEL if they choose the boat. Transport is separate; reduced categories are listed above.

### Is the boat ride included in the cave ticket?

No. Decide whether the optional supplement suits your budget, and confirm operation before buying it. The walking visit is a valid choice on its own.

### Is Prometheus Cave open on Mondays?

The current APA listing says daily, but an older official guide excludes Mondays. Confirm your specific date with the visitor centre before arranging transport.

### Can you visit Prometheus Cave in November?

It can fit a November itinerary, subject to the day's operating conditions. Bring a layer, check after heavy rain and keep a backup plan if the boat is unavailable.

### How much time should you allow for a visit?

We recommend reserving around two hours at the site, plus transport. This planning buffer allows for tickets and group timing rather than assuming you can arrive and immediately enter.
