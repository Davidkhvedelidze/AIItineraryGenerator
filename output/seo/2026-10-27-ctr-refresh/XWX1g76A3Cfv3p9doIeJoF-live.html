Kassland Georgia: Diamond Bridge Tickets, Hours & Tips

Kassland Georgia is the home of the Diamond Bridge, a 240-metre glass walkway above Tsalka Canyon, also called Dashbashi Canyon. For a visit from Tbilisi, the useful questions are what the ticket costs, which activities it covers and how much of the day to allow. This guide puts those decisions first, with official booking links and a practical plan for the canyon.

Prices and operating information checked on 27 October 2026. Recheck your chosen ticket and the weather before travelling.

Kassland tickets and opening hours: the quick answer

The canyon ticket page linked by Kass Land currently lists these categories:

  • Standard canyon ticket: 79 GEL.
  • Georgian citizen ticket: 46 GEL; check the identification required for this category.
  • Swing: 35 GEL.
  • Bikeline: 120 GEL.

The operator links to a separate Kass Land Attraction Park booking page. That page lists an unlimited attractions and giant-trampolines package at 59 GEL, a resident version at 35 GEL and an eight-minute adult go-kart session at 44 GEL.

These are different products. Check the inclusions for the exact ticket you select, especially bridge access, the canyon trail and any extra activity. Do not assume an attraction-park package includes the canyon, or that the standard canyon ticket includes every ride. Child eligibility and discounts need checking separately.

The operator's Plan Your Visit page lists the canyon and Glass Bridge & Diamond 360 as open daily, 10:00–19:00. It lists the bike line Monday–Saturday, 10:00–18:30. Treat those as the advertised hours, not a promise that every attraction will operate throughout your visit: ask about last admission, trail access and weather restrictions.

Where is the Diamond Bridge in Georgia?

The Diamond Bridge is at Kass Land in the Tsalka area, near Dashbashi village in the country of Georgia. It is not a bridge in central Tbilisi. Searches for Kassland, Kass Land, Tsalka Canyon and Dashbashi Canyon often refer to different parts of the same destination.

The operator's booking hub describes the drive as around 90 minutes from Tbilisi. For planning, allow roughly 1.5–2 hours each way, with extra time for city traffic, breaks or poor weather. Your departure address and the day's road conditions matter more than a single advertised journey time.

Save the venue's entrance location before leaving. A map pin for Tsalka town or a general canyon viewpoint may not take you to the correct visitor entrance. If using a driver, agree on the entrance and pickup point rather than asking only for “the canyon.”

Is Kassland worth visiting?

Kassland makes most sense when you specifically want the glass bridge, canyon scenery or paid adventure activities. It can provide a different kind of day between city sightseeing and winery visits. Choose it because those experiences appeal to your group, rather than assuming every Georgia itinerary needs the same stop.

It is a weaker fit if you dislike heights, have only a short stay in Tbilisi or want an inexpensive nature walk. The bridge is a major part of the appeal, and travel plus admission takes a meaningful share of the day and budget. Decide whether you would still enjoy the trip if a particular ride were unavailable.

For a family, ask each person what they actually want to do. One traveller may want the sky bike, another the canyon walk, and someone else may prefer a relaxed lunch. That conversation is more useful than buying every possible activity in advance.

Compare the destination with our best day trips from Tbilisi. A history-focused day and an adventure-park visit satisfy different interests; choose the one your group is most likely to enjoy.

How much should you budget for the day?

Start with transport, then add the exact ticket categories, optional activities and meals. Keeping these separate helps when comparing an independent visit with a tour quotation. A low transport price can look attractive until you discover that admission and waiting time are additional.

For two adults using the listed standard canyon category, the tickets alone total 158 GEL. If one adult adds the listed swing, that becomes 193 GEL before transport and food. This is an illustrative calculation from the prices above, not a bundled offer.

Do not multiply the adult price by the size of a family without checking children's categories first. Equally, do not assume a child can join a ride because there is a family ticket elsewhere in the park. Ask about the current height, age, weight and supervision rules for each activity.

When requesting transport, ask for a written total that makes clear whether it includes the return journey, waiting time, parking and any other stops. Our Georgia trip budget guide can help you decide how much to allocate to this day within the whole holiday.

Getting to Kassland from Tbilisi

Private driver

A private driver can make the day easier when travelling with children, combining different activities or wanting a flexible return time. Agree on how long the driver will wait and what happens if your visit runs late. Specify any child-seat needs before confirming.

For a first visit, a simple out-and-back plan is easier to manage than several widely separated stops. Tell the driver whether you intend to walk the canyon trail, since that affects the time you need on site. Our private-driver guide explains what to ask when comparing arrangements.

Rental car

Driving yourself gives you control over breaks and departure time. Before setting out, check the weather and route, save offline directions and allow time to find the correct entrance and parking. Choose a return time that leaves a comfortable margin, especially when daylight is limited.

Avoid treating the advertised journey time as a deadline. If the weather deteriorates, revise the plan rather than trying to recover time on unfamiliar roads. A day trip should leave room for a slower journey home.

Shared excursion or public transport

For a shared excursion, confirm the time at Kassland, ticket inclusions and cancellation arrangements. A description that says “Diamond Bridge tour” may refer to transport and a stop at the venue; inspect the inclusions before comparing its total with a self-planned visit.

If investigating public transport, verify both the outward service and the final connection to the visitor entrance, plus the return. Do not build the day around an unverified direct bus or assume a late return taxi will be available. This guide does not list a current confirmed public-transport timetable.

The bridge, canyon walk and extra attractions

Think of the visit as several choices rather than one continuous activity. The glass bridge is the signature viewpoint. The operator also advertises a nature trail, waterfalls, a sky bike, a swing and a separate attractions park.

Walking in the canyon is a different undertaking from crossing the bridge. Ask staff about the route, surface, steps, current access and the return climb before starting. Wear shoes with grip and carry water. Do not assume a canyon trail is step-free or suitable for a pushchair because the wider venue welcomes families.

If heights worry you, discuss the layout with staff before buying tickets. A ground-based activity may suit you better, but confirm how it is reached and whether the route itself involves exposure. There is no need for everyone in a group to choose the same optional ride.

A realistic day-trip plan

Here is a suggested sequence, not an operator timetable:

  • Leave Tbilisi in the morning, allowing for the journey and the venue's advertised opening time.
  • At arrival, confirm weather conditions, ticket inclusions and which activities are running.
  • Visit the bridge while keeping time for photographs and pauses.
  • Choose the canyon walk or a paid activity according to energy, comfort and conditions.
  • Take a meal break and allow a flexible return journey.

As a planning allowance, reserve about 2–4 hours on site, depending on walking pace, queues, meals and activities. With travel, that usually means setting aside most of the day. A quick bridge-only stop is different from a family visit with walking and rides.

For an autumn or winter trip, check daylight as well as the advertised closing time. A venue remaining open into the evening does not mean you will have the same light for canyon views or the same conditions for walking.

Visiting with children: what to check first

Before paying, confirm children's ticket categories, supervision requirements and restrictions for each ride. Bring layers and footwear appropriate for an outdoor day. Choose one priority activity, then treat anything else as optional.

For pushchairs, mobility limitations or a family member who tires easily, ask the venue which areas are accessible from the entrance and where rest stops are available. Keep the walking plan realistic for the least mobile person in your group.

Our Tbilisi with kids guide offers alternatives if an all-day excursion feels too ambitious. A shorter city activity may fit better after a late arrival or before an early flight.

Before you book

Check the canyon and attraction-park products separately, confirm the return transport and ask about the activities you care about most. Keep the booking confirmation accessible offline. If a weather-dependent ride is the reason for your visit, check its operating status before leaving Tbilisi.

Once you have those answers, Kassland becomes easier to place in an itinerary: one deliberately planned outdoor day, with a clear budget and enough time to enjoy it.

Frequently asked questions

How much are Kassland Diamond Bridge tickets?
The operator-linked canyon booking page listed a standard ticket at 79 GEL and a Georgian citizen ticket at 46 GEL when checked on 27 October 2026. The swing and bikeline were separate options at 35 GEL and 120 GEL. Confirm the selected ticket's bridge and trail inclusions before paying.
What are the Diamond Bridge opening hours?
Kass Land advertises the canyon and Glass Bridge & Diamond 360 as open daily from 10:00 to 19:00. Recheck last admission, trail access and weather-related restrictions before travelling.
Where is the Diamond Bridge, and how far is it from Tbilisi?
It is at Kass Land near Dashbashi village in the Tsalka area of Georgia. The operator advertises approximately 90 minutes from Tbilisi; allow around 1.5–2 hours each way as a planning estimate, depending on traffic, weather and your starting point.
Does a Kassland ticket include every attraction?
Do not assume so. The operator links to separate canyon and attraction-park products, with additional activities listed separately. Read the inclusions for the exact ticket or package you choose.
Is Kassland suitable for children or visitors afraid of heights?
It depends on the person and chosen activity. Check children's eligibility and ride restrictions with staff, and ask about access to the canyon trail. The glass bridge involves height exposure, and an outdoor trail should not be assumed to be step-free or pushchair-friendly.
